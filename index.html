Lane 01

Quantum computing, built to be checked.

Circuit design, simulation benches, and hybrid quantum-classical workflows — with evidence trails you can rerun. If a result can't be reproduced, it doesn't ship.

  • Simulator benchesCPU-first circuit demonstrations. The sequence corpus uses unseeded shots; rerun outcomes can vary.

Lane 02

Exchange infrastructure with guardrails.

Paper-first trading systems, approval ceremonies, and risk controls — designed so that nothing real moves without explicit human sign-off.

  • Paper-firstSimulated settlement and ledgers before a single real order is ever considered.
  • Approval ceremoniesTyped approvals, matching digests, and human verdicts on every action that matters.
  • Risk boundariesHard caps, session limits, and fail-closed defaults — the safe state is the default state.

How we work

Principles, not slogans.

  1. Verify before announcing.Independent checks beat confident claims. Every time.
  2. Simulators before hardware.Prove it cheap, then prove it real.
  3. Humans decide.Agents propose, people dispose. Verdicts belong to the owner.
  4. Fail closed.When in doubt, the system stops — it never guesses forward.
