Read [public progress](../PROGRESS.md) and [research status](../RESEARCH-STATUS.md) for what is published versus reported. The [DeepNet master site](https://agenci-main.github.io/deepnet-chat/) is related but outside this repository.

| This repo (`hybrid-quantum-exchange`) | public | Bottlenecks corpus, Erdős/Aer `LOCAL_SIM` lanes, product shell |

Hardware / paid cloud QPU work is gated. Default: **submit locked**. Labels: `LOCAL_SIM` · `CLOUD_SIM` · `REAL_QPU`.

## Evidence and claims

- `LOCAL_SIM` means simulator output, not a run on physical hardware. The [Erdős lanes](../research/quantum-erdos-sequences/README.md) check finite properties against classical answers; neither a PASS nor a lane count resolves the original open problems or demonstrates advantage.
- [Bottleneck entries](../bottlenecks/README.md) are source-grounded research notes with thematic coverage, not peer-reviewed findings or exhaustive literature reviews.
- Private-repository status cannot be independently checked from this public tree. The site is a UI shell, not evidence of a live exchange.
