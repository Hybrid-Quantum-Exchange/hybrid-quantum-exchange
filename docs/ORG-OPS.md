## Operating boundary

This repository documents public research and a product UI shell. Its presence
does not establish a production exchange, a live backend, QPU access, or an
operational integration. Simulation labels and results must remain identified
as `LOCAL_SIM`; they are not hardware results.

This docs-only status board authorizes no QPU work or submission, settings
changes, secrets handling, billing/IAM work, invitations, DeepNet runtime or
schema changes, or FIRE work. The default hardware posture remains submit
locked; no `REAL_QPU` run or unlock is claimed.

Do not report yield, return, fidelity, speedup, or quantum-advantage figures
unless the relevant result is supported by traceable evidence. No such figures
are established by this page or by the DeepNet link.
