# ORG-OPS guide

This is a public, documentation-only operating guide for the Hybrid Quantum Exchange repository. It describes boundaries; it does not grant organization access, establish operational readiness, or authorize account or infrastructure changes.

## Reference

The [DeepNet master documentation](https://agenci-main.github.io/deepnet-chat/) is an external reference only. This repository does not define or verify a DeepNet integration, runtime, schema, availability, or operational handoff.

## Allowed scope

- Update public documentation and indexes in this repository.
- Describe repository contents and checks with links to verifiable sources.
- Record CI evidence only when a run and its relevant artifacts are directly linked.
- Label simulator work `LOCAL_SIM`; distinguish documented procedures from completed runs.

## Explicitly out of scope

This guide does not authorize QPU submissions or unlocks, secrets handling, Settings changes, billing/IAM changes, invitations, DeepNet runtime/schema work, or FIRE work. Do not infer permission to access an organization, account, or external service from these documents.

## Reporting rules

- Do not invent yields, fidelity values, returns, or other measurements.
- Do not describe estimates or simulations as realized results or hardware results.
- Do not claim quantum advantage without reproducible evidence and a classical baseline.
- Keep the QPU posture at **Hold** unless an explicit, separately documented authorization and run receipts exist.

See [Honesty / QPU Hold](HONESTY-QPU-HOLD.md), [Public status](../STATUS.md), and the [CI evidence index](CI-EVIDENCE.md).
