# Public operations boundary

**Snapshot: 2026-09-30.** This page records the scope of this documentation-only update. It does not grant operational access or authorize changes to services or accounts.

## In scope

- Maintain public README, progress, status, honesty, and evidence-index documentation.
- Link the [DeepNet master](https://agenci-main.github.io/deepnet-chat/) as an external documentation reference only.
- Describe evidence that is present in this repository and clearly label evidence that is absent.

## Explicitly out of scope

- QPU or paid-cloud submissions, unlocks, credentials, or hardware operations.
- Secrets, Settings, billing/IAM, or invitations.
- DeepNet runtime, API, or schema changes, or claims of an integration.
- FIRE work.

Do not add yield, fidelity, speedup, or other measured-result claims without reproducible evidence and a suitable baseline. Simulator and paper results are not hardware results. See [Honesty / QPU Hold](HONESTY-QPU-HOLD.md), [public status](../STATUS.md), and [CI evidence](CI-EVIDENCE.md).
