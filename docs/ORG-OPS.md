simulation as `LOCAL_SIM`; do not present simulation as hardware execution,
quantum advantage, or financial yield. No yield or fidelity numbers are
established by this page.

NO invites, NO DeepNet runtime/schema, NO FIRE**. Do not add credentials or imply
that this public repository controls private systems.
